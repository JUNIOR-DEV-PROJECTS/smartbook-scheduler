# SmartBook Scheduler

Build "Smart Scheduling for Modern Businesses", a multi-tenant appointment scheduling and business management SaaS for appointment-based businesses (salons, clinics, spas, practices). Include authentication, business onboarding wizard, dashboard, calendar (day/week/month) with double-booking prevention, services and staff availability management, customer CRM, public business profile and mobile-first booking flow (/book/[business-slug]), customer appointment portal, notification architecture, and subscription billing structure (Starter $19, Pro $39, Business $79).
